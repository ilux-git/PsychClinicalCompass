What to do:

Models (app/modules/assessments/models.py):

QuestionnaireResponse (id, patient_id, form_version, score, risk_flags, created_at).

OutboxEvent (id, event_type, payload_json, status [pending/processed], created_at) — table for the Outbox pattern.

Business Logic (app/modules/assessments/service.py):

API (app/modules/assessments/router.py):

POST /assessments/responses — endpoint to save responses (accessible only to the patient role).
